踝关节与足部损伤 Ankle & Foot Injuries

篮球运动发病率第一（>40%） Most Common Basketball Injury (>40%)

外侧踝关节扭伤（崴脚） Inversion Ankle Sprain ("Rolled Ankle")

实拍示意：踝关节外侧距腓前韧带防伤贴扎与评估 Photo: Lateral Ankle Ligament Assessment & Taping

发生原因Causes

拼抢篮板落地踩到他人脚背、防守急停变向时足部剧烈内翻，导致外侧距腓前韧带被过度拉伸甚至撕裂。 Landing on another player's foot or sudden cutting movements causing severe ankle inversion, stretching or tearing the anterior talofibular ligament.

预防方法Prevention

  • 穿戴具备良好侧向支撑的球鞋或高阶运动护踝。Wear supportive basketball shoes or high-grade ankle braces.
  • 日常进行单脚平衡站立、平衡垫训练以增强踝关节本体感觉。Perform single-leg balance and wobble board exercises to improve proprioception.

科学康复步骤Rehabilitation Protocol

  1. 急性期：Acute: 实施 POLICE 保护与冷敷，消肿镇痛。Apply P.O.L.I.C.E protocol to manage pain and swelling.
  2. 恢复期：Recovery: 踝关节字母写字训练，恢复主动关节活动度。Ankle alphabet exercises for ROM.
  3. 强化期：Strengthening: 弹力带抗阻内外翻训练、提踵增强小腿力量。Resistance band eversion/inversion & calf raises.

跟腱炎 Achilles Tendonitis

实拍示意：小腿后肌群与跟腱物理放松与离心训练 Photo: Calf & Achilles Tendon Physical Therapy & Eccentric Exercise

发生原因Causes

反复高强度弹跳起跳、缺乏充分热身，或小腿比目鱼肌与腓肠肌过度紧绷，导致跟腱附着点承受过高张力。 Repeated jumping, insufficient warm-up, or tight gastrocnemius/soleus muscles causing excessive tension on the Achilles tendon.

预防方法Prevention

  • 训练前后充分进行小腿肌肉深层拉伸与筋膜放松。Perform deep calf stretching & foam rolling before and after games.
  • 避免突然大幅增加训练负荷与高频弹跳量。Avoid sudden spikes in training volume or jump drills.

科学康复步骤Rehabilitation Protocol

  1. 离心训练：Eccentric Exercise: 台阶离心提踵训练，缓慢降低脚跟。Eccentric heel drops on a step, slowly lowering the heel.
  2. 筋膜梳理：Myofascial Release: 滚球放松足底筋膜，降低跟腱整体张力。Plantar fascia ball rolling to relieve overall strain.
部位 02Region 02

膝关节损伤 Knee Joint Injuries

高风险重点防护区 High-Risk Impact Zone

前交叉韧带损伤 Anterior Cruciate Ligament (ACL) Tear

实拍示意：膝关节前交叉韧带医学触诊与评估 Photo: Medical ACL Palpation & Physical Examination

发生原因Causes

急停急转或单腿落地时出现膝关节内扣塌陷，股骨与胫骨发生剧烈扭转，拉力超出韧带最大生理极限。 Knee valgus collapse during sudden cutting or single-leg landing, exceeding physiological limits of the ACL.

预防方法Prevention

  • 建立正确的落地姿势机制：双腿屈膝屈髋缓冲，避免膝盖向内塌陷。Train proper jump-landing mechanics (hip & knee flexion, avoiding valgus).
  • 加强臀中肌与腘绳肌力量，提升膝关节动态稳定性。Strengthen gluteus medius and hamstrings to enhance stability.

科学康复步骤Rehabilitation Protocol

严重撕裂通常需要手术重建。术后需经历 6-9 个月的系统物理治疗，包含膝关节屈伸度重建、股四头肌激活与神经肌肉控制训练。 Surgical reconstruction required for severe tears, followed by 6-9 months of physical therapy focused on range of motion, quad activation, and neuromuscular control.

髌骨肌腱炎（跳跃膝） Patellar Tendonitis ("Jumper's Knee")

实拍示意：膝盖髌腱防护与下肢力量锻炼 Photo: Patellar Tendon Protection & Lower Limb Exercise

发生原因Causes

高频起跳与落地缓冲使股四头肌产生强大收缩力，集中拉扯髌骨下方髌腱，形成无菌性微小损伤累积。 Repetitive jumping and landing creating high forces through the quadriceps tendon, leading to micro-tears in the patellar tendon.

预防方法Prevention

  • 使用髌骨加压带分担髌腱承载的张力。Use a patellar strap to unload pressure on the tendon.
  • 充分滚压放松股四头肌与髂胫束，降低肌腱张力。Foam roll quads and IT bands regularly.

科学康复步骤Rehabilitation Protocol

  1. 等长收缩：Isometrics: 靠墙静蹲保持 45 秒，有效阻断疼痛信号。Wall sits (45-sec holds) for pain relief.
  2. 斜板下蹲：Decline Squats: 利用 25 度斜板做单腿下蹲，重构肌腱纤维耐受力。Single-leg decline board squats to rebuild tendon tolerance.
部位 03Region 03

肩部与上肢损伤 Shoulder & Upper Body Injuries

抢篮板与封盖高发区 Rebounding & Blocking Impact Zone

肩袖拉伤与肩关节脱位 Rotator Cuff Strain & Shoulder Dislocation

发生原因Causes

手臂高举封盖或抢球时受到外力强行拉扯，引起冈上肌等肩袖肌腱拉伤，极端撞击下可致肱骨头脱出关节盂。 Forced overhead arm pulling while blocking or rebounding leading to supraspinatus strain or glenohumeral dislocation.

预防方法Prevention

强化肩袖肌群（冈上肌、冈下肌、小圆肌、肩胛下肌）的弹力带小重量抗阻稳定性练习。 Strengthen rotator cuff muscles with resistance band internal/external rotations.

康复训练Rehabilitation

脱位后需先进行专业复位。恢复期采用姿势训练与弹力带内外旋恢复肩膀旋转力量。 Professional reduction required for dislocation. Perform Y-T-W-L drills and light band rotations during rehab.

部位 04Region 04

手腕与手指损伤 Wrist & Hand Injuries

球场高频急救类损伤 Frequent On-Court Trauma

手指关节挫伤（戳手指） Finger Jam / Collateral Ligament Sprain

成因：Causes: 篮球正面快速撞击伸直的指尖，导致指间侧副韧带过伸损伤或肌腱止点撕裂。 Direct impact of basketball on extended fingertips, causing joint hyper-extension or extensor tendon tear.

预防：Prevention: 接球时保持手掌张开、手指自然放松缓冲。 Maintain relaxed hands and proper ball-catching technique.

应急：First Aid: 立即冰敷，使用邻指固定法（将伤指与邻近健康手指用医用胶布包扎固定）。 Apply ice immediately and use Buddy Taping (tape damaged finger to adjacent healthy finger).

手腕扭伤 Wrist Sprain

成因：Causes: 失衡跌倒时用单手剧烈撑地，手腕背伸承受全身体重过度压迫。 Landing forcefully on an outstretched hand during a fall, hyperextending wrist ligaments.

预防：Prevention: 练习安全跌倒翻滚保护技巧，避免直臂硬撑。 Practice break-fall rolling techniques; avoid landing on stiff straight arms.

应急：First Aid: 佩戴护腕固定 1-2 周，后期进行握力与手腕屈伸力量恢复。 Immobilize with wrist brace for 1-2 weeks, followed by wrist flexor/extensor strengthening.

部位 05Region 05

腰背部与核心损伤 Lower Back & Core Injuries

动力链发力传输轴心 Core Kinetic Chain Axis

急性腰肌拉伤与核心失稳 Lumbar Strain & Core Instability

成因分析：Causes:

弯腰抢球或空中碰撞时受到突发剪力，或深层核心肌肉未激活导致腰椎肌肉过载代偿。 Sudden shearing forces during rebound battles or unengaged deep core leading to lumbar muscle strain.

预防策略：Prevention:

强化平板支撑、鸟狗式核心稳定训练；发力时保持脊柱中立。 Perform planks and Bird-Dog core stability drills; maintain neutral spine alignment.

康复路径：Rehabilitation:

急性期后适度进行腰椎伸展练习，拉伸放松髂腰肌与腘绳肌。 McKenzie extension exercises after acute phase; stretch psoas and hamstrings.

心理康复与心态调适Mental Rehabilitation & Mindset

伤病心理调适：重返赛场的隐形钥匙 Mental Recovery: The Hidden Key to Returning to Play

作为篮球运动员，受伤不仅是对身体的考验，更是对心理韧性的磨炼。积极调适心理状态能显著促进身体的生理愈合。 As an athlete, injury tests both physical and mental resilience. Maintaining a positive mindset significantly accelerates physiological healing.

01

接纳情绪，避免焦虑 Accept Emotions & Reduce Anxiety

受伤后产生沮丧和对无法上场的焦虑是完全正常的。接纳现状是复出的第一步，将注意力从“我失去了什么”转移到“我现在能做哪些康复”。 Feeling frustrated after an injury is normal. Acceptance is step one: shift focus from what was lost to what rehab actions can be taken today.

02

制定阶段性微目标 Set Micro Goals

把目标拆解到每天：今天消肿了 1 厘米、今天完成了 3 组腿抬高。每一个微小的进步都会重新建立起你对身体的掌控感。 Break rehab down daily: swelling reduction, range of motion gain, or quad lifts. Small wins rebuild confidence and control over your body.

03

保持与球队连接 Stay Connected with Your Team

即使不能上场，也可以坐在场边观战、为队友加油或帮忙记录技术统计。保持团队归属感能极大地消除孤立无助感。 Even while injured, stay involved by cheering from the bench or keeping stats. Belonging to the team mitigates feelings of isolation.

急性伤病应急处置Emergency First Aid Protocol

急性运动损伤应急原则（POLICE 原则） Acute Injury First Aid Protocol: P.O.L.I.C.E

发生扭伤或拉伤后的前 24-48 小时，科学遵循 POLICE 原则替代传统的静止不动，能有效保护组织并加速早期修复。 During the first 24-48 hours after sprains or strains, the P.O.L.I.C.E protocol protects tissue while promoting optimal healing.

P

保护 (Protect) Protect

立即停止运动，佩戴护具或支架固定伤处，防止二次受力撕裂。 Stop playing immediately; use braces or splints to prevent further tissue tearing.

OL

适当负重 (Optimal Loading) Optimal Loading

在无明显剧痛的前提下进行早期微活动，促进韧带与肌肉纤维重塑。 Gentle, pain-free movement encourages early tissue remodeling and strength.

I

冰敷 (Ice) Ice

冰敷 15-20 分钟/次，控制局部炎性渗出，减缓神经传导痛感。 Apply cold packs 15-20 mins per session to manage acute pain and swelling.

C

加压 (Compression) Compression

使用弹性绷带适当加压包扎，限制组织液积聚，减轻肿胀疼痛。 Wrap with elastic bandages to control fluid accumulation and swelling.

E

抬高 (Elevation) Elevation

将受伤肢体抬高至高于心脏平面的高度，利用重力促进血液回流。 Elevate injured limb above heart level to assist venous drainage.
